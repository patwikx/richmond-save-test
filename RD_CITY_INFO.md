PEZA Fiscal Incentives (2025)

Income Tax Holiday (ITH): 4-7 years exemption from corporate income tax
Special Corporate Income Tax (SCIT): 5% tax on gross income (vs. standard 25%) after ITH
VAT Zero-Rating: VAT-exempt local purchases for registered businesses
Exemption from Local Taxes: Exempt from local government taxes, licenses, and fees (except real property tax)
Enhanced Deductions: Additional deductions for buildings, machinery, direct labor, R&D, and employee training

PEZA Non-Fiscal Incentives

One-Stop Shop: Streamlined process for permits and licenses
Transparent Procedures: Efficient and transparent business processes
Visa Processing Assistance: Support for foreign nationals
Access to Infrastructure & Utilities: Comprehensive ecosystem including utility management, security, and transportation
Simplified Import/Export: On-site customs offices and special processing lanes at ports and airports
